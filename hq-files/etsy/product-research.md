# Etsy Product Research — Night 1

## Research Methodology
Based on public Etsy search patterns for high-performing printable/template niches.

## Selected Products (3)

### Product 1: College Student Budget Planner
**Why this wins:**
- Huge audience: 20+ million US college students
- Price range on Etsy: $2–$8 for printables, $5–$9 for Notion templates
- Best sellers have 500–3000+ reviews
- Low competition at the $4–6 price point for quality designs
- Evergreen: sells year-round, spikes in August and January (semester starts)

**Our product:** 12-page PDF printable budget planner, US Letter
- Monthly budget overview
- Weekly spending tracker
- Subscription tracker (what am I paying monthly?)
- "Broke or just bad at budgeting?" audit worksheet
- Semester expense planner (books, housing, fees)
- Savings goal tracker
- Grocery list template
- Side hustle income log

**Price:** $4.99
**Target keywords:** college budget planner printable, broke college student, student budget worksheet, semester budget planner, monthly budget printable

### Product 2: Small Business Invoice & Quote Kit
**Why this wins:**
- Serves the exact same local businesses you already email
- Price range on Etsy: $3–$15 for templates
- Best sellers (cleaning, landscaping, handyman niches) have 1000+ reviews
- Buyers often come back for customized versions → repeat revenue
- You can point your email leads to this kit as a "free download" in exchange for a lead

**Our product:** 8-page editable PDF kit
- Professional invoice template (fill-in fields)
- Service quote/estimate template
- Payment terms cheat sheet (Net 15, Net 30 explained simply)
- "How to follow up on unpaid invoices" one-pager
- Printable receipt template
- Business expense tracker sheet

**Price:** $5.99
**Target keywords:** small business invoice template, handyman invoice, cleaning business invoice, service business quote template, contractor estimate template

---

### Product 3: Notion Student Dashboard Template
**Why this wins:**
- Notion templates are a fast-growing Etsy category
- Price range: $3–$15, bestsellers often $5–$8
- College students actively look for Notion setups
- Can be delivered as a file download (Notion template link in a PDF)
- Cross-promotes to the budget planner

**Our product:** Notion template with 5 linked views:
- Semester overview (courses, assignments, deadlines)
- Weekly schedule builder
- Grade tracker
- Study session log
- Budget tracker (links to the budget planner)

**Delivery:** PDF with instructions + the Notion template duplicate link

**Price:** $3.99
**Target keywords:** Notion student template, college Notion dashboard, student planner Notion, free Notion college template, Notion academic planner
