# Google Business Profile setup and cleanup ($75 one-time)

Idea bank #1. A small, quick sale for businesses that don't need a new website yet,
or a foot in the door before a site or care plan pitch.

## What the $75 covers
- Check that name, address, phone, hours and website on the profile are correct
- Pick the right primary and extra categories
- Write a plain business description (750 characters max, Google's limit)
- Add services or a menu link, and holiday hours if any are coming up
- Upload up to 10 photos the owner sends me
- One short written note: what I changed and what to keep up

## What I can't do for them (say this up front)
- Google Business Profile is free from Google. They're paying for my time, not for the profile.
- I can't verify the profile for them. Google sends verification to the owner (video, phone,
  or postcard, depending on the business), so the owner has to do that step.
- I won't log in to their Google account. They add me as a Manager on the profile
  (Profile > Business Profile settings > People and access > Add), and they can remove me after.
- No promises about ranking higher. A complete, accurate profile helps, but Google decides.

## Who to send it to
Leads where the website status is "None" or "Broken", or where Google Maps shows missing
hours, no photos, or no description. Pairs well with the "website rescue" idea (#19).

## First email (use the dashboard's normal draft + approval flow)

Subject: Quick fix for {{business}}'s Google listing

Hi {{name}},

I was looking up {{business}} on Google Maps and noticed {{one real, specific thing, e.g. "the hours aren't listed" or "the website link doesn't load"}}.

I'm a local web designer near Greenville. For $75 one-time I'll clean up your Google Business Profile: correct hours and info, the right categories, a short description, and your photos. You stay the owner; you just add me as a manager and can remove me after.

Want me to take a look? If not, reply "no thanks" and I won't email again.

— Ghosttt

Rules for this template:
- Only send it when you've actually seen the problem you mention. Never make one up.
- If you can't name a real problem, don't send this one; use the normal website email.
